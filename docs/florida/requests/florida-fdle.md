**To:** publicrecords@fdle.state.fl.us
**Subject:** Public records request — Criminal Justice Data Transparency data
catalog, field documentation, and clerk-case data

To the Records Custodian:

Under Article I, section 24 of the Florida Constitution and chapter 119, Florida
Statutes, I request the records below about the Criminal Justice Data
Transparency (CJDT) database that sections 900.05 and 943.6871, Florida
Statutes, establish. I make the request for JudgeMetrics, a public-interest
project that publishes reproducible statistics on criminal-court outcomes by
court and by judge, with its methodology and the source of every figure.

**1. The data catalog.** The data catalog or data dictionaries described in
rule 11C-11.001, Florida Administrative Code, for the clerk of court data, and
the field list of each public "Full Data Download" dataset on the CJDT pages
(clerk case, state attorney case, county detention arrest, incarceration and
supervision, and scoresheet): column names, definitions, and code values.

**2. Specific fields.** Records showing:

a. which column of the public clerk-case dataset holds the unique identifier
   that section 943.6871(1) requires, and whether the same identifier appears in
   the other public datasets;
b. which columns hold the sentencing judge (section 900.05(3)(a)18.), the
   first-appearance pretrial release determination (14.a.), and the date of any
   failure to appear (15.b.);
c. whether the public data identifies the judicial officer who made a pretrial
   release determination or entered a charge disposition.

**3. Completeness.** Records of clerk-of-court reporting completeness by county
(for example compliance or data-quality reports), including the earliest month
each clerk's data covers — in particular for Hillsborough, Broward, and
Miami-Dade counties.

**4. Access and cadence.** The documentation of the application program
interface that section 943.6871(3) requires, and of the update schedule of the
public datasets.

**5. Removals.** Records describing how a record that becomes sealed, expunged,
or otherwise confidential after publication is removed from the public datasets,
and whether removals are identified in the published data.

**6. Clerk-case data, if not in the public download.** Only if the public
clerk-case dataset omits the unique identifier or the sentencing judge: the
clerk-case data for criminal cases filed on or after January 1, 2018 in
Hillsborough, Broward, and Miami-Dade counties with those two fields, the case
number, charges, charge dispositions with their dates and types, the
first-appearance pretrial release determinations, failure-to-appear dates, and
sentences, as CSV with a header row and its layout, with monthly updates if
available. Please exclude everything that is confidential or exempt under
section 900.05(6) and other law — including sealed and expunged records and
juvenile records — and include no name, date of birth, address, race, or sex:
the unique identifier is the only person field I ask for.

**7. Cost.** I understand the department waives the first 30 minutes. Please do
not undertake any work beyond those 30 minutes without first sending me a
written estimate and receiving my written approval; I do not authorize any
charge otherwise. If part 6 would require a charge, please fulfil parts 1–5
and 8 first.

**8. Use and redistribution.** Section 943.6871(3) says that "The department
may not require a license or charge a fee to access or receive information from
the database." Please state in writing whether the department places any
condition on the use of the public CJDT data or of records provided under part
6, and specifically whether I may:

1. republish derived aggregates — counts, rates, and statistics by judge, court,
   and period that reproduce no individual record;
2. republish pseudonymous case-level views — a case's events, charges,
   dispositions, and sentences under a random key, without any person's name,
   date of birth, address, or identifier;
3. redistribute either of the above commercially, for example in a licensed
   research dataset or a paid data service.

**Delivery.** Electronic copies as maintained (PDF, spreadsheet, or text), by
email or HTTPS download.
